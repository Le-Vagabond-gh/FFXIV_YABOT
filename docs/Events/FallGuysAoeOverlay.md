# Fall Guys Stage 3 AoE Markers

On the last stage of the Fall Guys collaboration event, outlines the AoEs that are about to go off (about 2.5 seconds ahead) and draws a safe route through the lanes.

- **Markers**: yellow outlines for upcoming AoEs. A marker turns red when your current movement (or standing still) would put you inside it as it fires.
- **Route**: a green line from your character through the lanes to the finish. It appears once the plugin has seen the opening exaflares and the rect lanes fire, and updates as you move down the course.

Only stage 3 is covered. Ported from [awgil/ffxiv_vfallguy](https://github.com/awgil/ffxiv_vfallguy) by veyn.
