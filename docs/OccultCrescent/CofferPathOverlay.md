# Occult Crescent Coffer Path Overlay

When the area map is open in South Horn or North Horn, overlays the community minimap-chest farming paths on top of it.

- **South Horn** (territory 1252): red NW loop (26 chests) and blue E/S loop (42 chests), individually toggleable.
- **North Horn** (territory 1346): five paths matching the wiki image colors - yellow (10 chests), blue (16), purple (21), green (13), orange (8) - behind a single toggle. Each path starts at an aetheryte (cyan diamond) and some include terrain-navigation bend points. Links to optional chests are drawn semi-transparent. The purple path ends with 6 chests in the North Subterrane; they are projected onto the surface map like the wiki image does, and shown on their own when the North Subterrane map (map 1244) is displayed.

Bronze chests render as copper dots, silver chests as lighter dots. Chest data is taken from EurekaTrackerAutoPopper's `OccultChests.cs` and the visit order matches the consolegameswiki Coffer Path images. Line thickness, dot size, and opacity are configurable.
