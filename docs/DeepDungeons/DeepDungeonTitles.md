# Zone Titles

Pick a title for each deep dungeon. It's applied when you walk into the area around the deep dungeon's entrance, and again inside the deep dungeon itself. Your previous title isn't restored when you leave.

| Deep dungeon | Entrance area |
|---|---|
| Palace of the Dead | Quarrymill (South Shroud) |
| Heaven-on-High | Crick (The Ruby Sea) |
| Eureka Orthos | The Eight Sentinels (Mor Dhona) |
| Pilgrim's Traverse / Final Verse | Wolekdorf (Il Mheg) |

## Options

One dropdown per deep dungeon, listing the titles earned there plus the Accursed Hoard titles, which count across every deep dungeon. Titles you haven't unlocked yet are shown greyed out. Pick "(don't change)" to leave your title alone for that deep dungeon.

There is also an option to announce the title change in chat.

## PvP zones

Tick "Wear a Makai title in PvP zones" to put on a title from the GARO collaboration whenever you enter a PvP zone (Wolves' Den Pier, Crystalline Conflict, Frontline, Rival Wings) or change job there. It picks the Makai title of your current job if you own it, then Makai Master, then any other Makai title you own. If you own none, your title is left alone. Off by default.

If the titles aren't greyed out yet, open the game's title window once so the game loads your unlocked titles.
