# Keep Weapon in Deep Dungeons

Deep dungeons swap your weapon's appearance for a dungeon-specific model. This feature keeps the weapon you walked in with instead - the same model, glamour and dyes you had outside.

Both hands are covered: the main hand, and the off hand whether it's a real one (a Paladin's shield) or one bundled with the main hand that the character sheet doesn't list (a Machinist's aetherotransformer, for example).

Only the appearance changes. Your stats and actual gear are untouched.

## Options

Each deep dungeon has its own toggle, all on by default:

- Palace of the Dead
- Heaven-on-High
- Eureka Orthos
- Pilgrim's Traverse (also covers the Final Verse)

## Notes

- The weapon kept is the last one you had on outside a deep dungeon. If the plugin is loaded or reloaded while you're already inside, the dungeon model stays until you leave.
- A Glamourer design that sets your weapon still wins. If Glamourer doesn't touch your weapon, reverting to game state in Glamourer shows your kept weapon, not the dungeon model.
